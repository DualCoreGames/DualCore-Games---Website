Reclairos — The world is broken. Reclaim it, one wave at a time.

A 2D story-driven Tower Defense RPG for Android & iOS. Assemble your elite squad, protect the escort, and fight through a fractured world — before it's too late.

Your Commanders

Assemble Your Elite Squad: Tactical RPG Class Synergy

Five distinct combat roles. Infinite tactical combinations. Encounters are designed so no single unit type dominates — strategy demands synergy.

🛡️
Vanguard

Frontline Tank

High HP. Absorbs damage and slows the enemy advance. Buys critical time for ranged units to operate.

🏹
Ranger

Ranged DPS

Consistent damage from a safe distance. Vulnerable to fast disruptors that bypass the frontline.

✨
Warden

Support / Healer

Sustains the frontline with buffs and heals. Critical in grueling multi-wave encounters.

💥
Breaker

Burst / AoE Damage

Devastating burst output against grouped enemies. Long cooldown. Obliterates dense wave clusters.

⚡
Sentinel

Crowd Control

Applies slows, stuns, and traps. Counters fast disruptors. Low DPS, maximum utility.

Meet the Resistance

Command the Army: Ally Units & Warlords

Unlock and recruit legendary fighters as you reclaim lost territory. Filter by unit tier to inspect their strategic capabilities.

Basic Ally Unit

The Hitter

Single Attacker • Cheap to Spawn

Hitters are ordinary villagers who refused to watch their homeland fall into darkness. With no special training or heavy armor, they joined the Prince’s army with simple weapons.

Gameplay Role A low-cost single-target attacker with a fast respawn time. Hitters can quickly return to battle and support the frontline, becoming more effective when deployed in large groups.
Story Appearance One of the first allies available in the early levels of Reclairos, representing the courage of common people rising together with the Prince to take back their kingdom.
Basic Ally Unit

The Knight

Single Attacker • Medium Tanky

The Knights were once the loyal defenders of towns across Reclairos. After the demon invasion scattered the royal forces, these warriors returned to support the Prince.

Gameplay Role A strong and durable frontline unit designed to hold enemy attacks. With higher toughness than early fighters, Knights protect weaker allies behind them.
Story Appearance Unlocked after clearing Level 1, the Knight becomes one of the Prince’s first major reinforcements in his journey to rebuild the kingdom’s army.
Basic Ally Unit

Hawk Eye

Single Target • Medium Range

The Hawk Eyes were skilled archers who once protected the castle walls of Reclairos. Known for their sharp vision and accuracy, they strike threats before they reach the gates.

Gameplay Role A basic ranged unit that attacks enemies from a safe distance. Using precise arrow shots, the Hawk Eye supports frontline warriors by damaging enemies from behind.
Story Appearance After joining the Prince’s army, the Hawk Eye brings back the kingdom’s ranged forces and helps strengthen the fight to reclaim the lost territories of Reclairos.
Elite Ally Unit

Armored Axeman

Area Damage • Super Tanky

Once a mighty gate guard of the King’s Castle, trusted to protect the kingdom’s strongest defenses. Captured by demons, he was rescued by the Prince to join the fight.

Gameplay Role An elite tank unit with high durability and area damage. Armed with a massive axe, the Armored Axeman can hold back groups on the frontline and crush multiple opponents.
Story Appearance Rescued and unlocked after completing Level 3, the Armored Axeman becomes a key member of the Prince’s growing army, marking the return of the kingdom’s elite warriors.
Elite Ally Unit

Fire Knight

High Area Damage

The strongest elite soldier of the kingdom, a legendary warrior carrying a powerful fire-infused sword. Captive to the demon forces until the Prince broke his chains.

Gameplay Role An elite area-damage unit specializing in destroying groups of enemies. With his blazing sword strikes, the Fire Knight can unleash massive damage across the battlefield.
Story Appearance Rescued and unlocked after completing Level 6, the Fire Knight’s return marks a turning point in the reclamation journey as one of the greatest champions joins the fight.
Elite Ally Unit

Guardian Knight

Fast • Single Target

Fearless cavalry warriors who once charged at the frontline during the kingdom’s greatest wars. Riding powerful war horses and armed with deadly spears.

Gameplay Role An elite high-DPS single-target attacker with great speed and strength. The Guardian Knight can quickly reach the frontline and deliver powerful spear attacks.
Story Appearance After joining the Prince’s army, the return of the Guardian Knights restores the kingdom’s legendary cavalry force, bringing speed, power, and battlefield dominance.
Warlord Ally Unit

The Priest

Supporter • Area • Long Range

The spiritual guardian of the kingdom’s church, guiding and protecting the people. He joined the Prince’s army to use his sacred powers to save lives on the battlefield.

Gameplay Role A Warlord support unit specializing in ranged area healing. Instead of attacking, the Priest restores health to nearby allies, allowing them to hold their ground longer.
Story Appearance Unlocked after completing the Domain of Nerrisa event. The Priest’s arrival brings sacred healing power to the Prince’s forces, helping the army withstand stronger enemies.
Emperor Ally Unit

The King

Long Range • Massive Area Damage • Tanky

The legendary ruler of the Reclairos Empire. Captured by Ashrak Doomcaller, leaving the empire unprotected. Now freed, the King returns to fight beside the Prince.

Gameplay Role An Emperor-tier unit specializing in long-range, massive area damage. With his ability to control powerful crystals, the King strikes entire groups from great distances.
Story Appearance Unlocked after completing the main story mode. The King’s return represents the restoration of the Reclairos Empire and the reunion of the kingdom’s greatest heroes.

Threat Assessment

Reclairos Enemy Archive: Forces of Darkness

Study the demonic legions led by Ashrak Doomcaller. Filter by creature tier or threat level to prepare your strategy.

Basic Monster

Goblin

Single Target Attack

Goblins are lower-ranked creatures serving under Ignar’s command. They carry out the demon army’s basic missions, attacking villages and following orders from stronger monsters.

Gameplay Threat A fast-spawning minion. While low in health, they can quickly overwhelm your defenses if ignored or deployed in large numbers.
Story & Lore Small compared to other creatures, but still far stronger than an ordinary human, representing the core frontline infantry of Ignar's raiding parties.
Flying Demon Creature

Evil Eye

Area Damage Attack

A common but dangerous creature within the demon army. Evil Eyes attack from above, creating chaos in battle by striking warriors from the sky and weakening defenses before the main forces arrive.

Gameplay Threat Aerial attacker capable of bypassing heavy frontline defenses to directly target support units and escort characters.
Story & Lore Many underestimate them, but ignoring the skies has led countless armies to defeat. They serve as scouting beasts for the commanders.
Electric Warrior

Axeltronic

Single Target Attack

Born from ancient storms and forgotten lightning energy, Axeltronic is a monster formed from pure electricity. His body carries violent currents powerful enough to destroy anyone who stands against him.

Gameplay Threat Strikes with deadly precision and overwhelming electric force. His attacks stun single targets and leave defenders temporarily powerless.
Story & Lore Known as one of Ignar’s strongest warriors, he is channeled to break down fortified gates and heavily armored knights.
Ancient Heavy Guardian

Boulder Guy

Area Damage Attack

The unstoppable protector chosen to guard Ashrak Doomcaller himself. Do not judge Boulder Guy by his slow movement; his blade is capable of crushing whole columns of defenders.

Gameplay Threat Extremely high defensive parameters. Deals heavy cleaving area damage. Requires concentrated ranged fire to take down.
Story & Lore Legends say those who faced him never lived long enough to witness his sword swing. His presence alone was enough to make villages surrender.
Orc Warrior

Elite Orc

Area Control Attack

Elite Orcs are among the strongest fighters of the Orc clans, trained for destruction and conquest. They lead brutal raids across the lands, capturing villages, hunting humans, and spreading fear.

Gameplay Threat A dangerous frontline brawler. Knocks back friendly units and controls the field of combat with sweeping hammer attacks.
Story & Lore Under the command of the demon army, they serve as the shock troops responsible for breaking down fortified human towns.
Orc Commander

Orc Rider

Heavy Assault Attack

A powerful evolution of the Orc warriors, the Orc Rider stands above ordinary members of his clan. Known for leading swift cavalry invasions to expand the reach of darkness.

Gameplay Threat Fast charging unit that bypasses early setups. He commands local Orc squads, boosting their attack parameters in battle.
Story & Lore When an Orc Rider appears, war has already reached the gates. They act as vanguard commanders for Ashrak's operations.
Demon Commander

Ignar

Massive Destruction Attack

The right hand of Ashrak Doomcaller. Created from smokeless fire, Ignar became one of the most feared beings in history. His name alone was enough to spread terror across kingdoms.

Gameplay Threat Immune to basic steel weapons. Melts physical defenses and burns entire lanes with massive area-of-effect heat waves.
Story & Lore Blades could not harm him, and metal weapons melted before they could reach his burning body. Ignar was not sent to fight battles; he was sent to erase them.
Mythical Entity

The Ancient Guardian

Unknown Attack Pattern

Older than the kingdoms. Older than the wars. The Ancient Guardian belongs to no army and follows no ruler. He does not serve Ashrak, demons, or mankind.

Gameplay Threat A legendary boss unit. Attack patterns and vulnerabilities are completely undocumented, making encounters extremely high-risk.
Story & Lore Hidden beyond the known world, this sleeping legend protects the gateway to the unseen realm. Those who disturb his rest seek survival, not victory.

World Exploration: Episodic Narrative Chapters
