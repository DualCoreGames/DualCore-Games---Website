01  /  Project Overview

Shaders, Scripting, and Optimization

The Challenge

Standard engines introduce bloated asset loading cycles, heavy material setups, and high vertex overheads. The challenge was building lightweight rendering procedures that yield cinematic environments on constrained platforms.

The Solution

We authored HLSL shaders, developed custom procedural mesh generation modules, and programmed modular script pipelines. These tools bypass engine limitations and fit high-fidelity textures into narrow visual budgets.
