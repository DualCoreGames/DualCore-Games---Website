Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

DualCore Games ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at dualcoregames.com and when you use our mobile game Reclairos (Android). Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide (Website)

When you fill out a contact form, request a beta invite, or subscribe to our newsletter on our website, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Company name and project details (B2B enquiries)
  • Budget range and message content
  • Device platform preference (Android / iOS / Both)

1.2 Information Collected Automatically (Website)

When you browse our website, we and our third-party partners automatically collect certain technical data:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, referring URLs
  • Clicks, scroll depth, and mouse movement patterns (heatmaps)
  • Cookie identifiers and session data

1.3 Information Collected by Reclairos (Mobile App)

When you play Reclairos on Android, we and our integrated SDKs may collect:

  • Account data: Name and email address provided during beta registration
  • Device data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and advertising ID
  • Gameplay data: Session duration, levels played, in-game actions, progression, and performance metrics
  • Performance data: Frame rate, crash logs, error reports, and diagnostic information
  • Network data: IP address and connection type (Wi-Fi / mobile data)

We do not collect financial information, precise GPS location, microphone data, camera data, or contacts from your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to your business enquiries and deliver requested services
  • Grant and manage access to the Reclairos beta programme
  • Send beta updates, patch notes, and feedback requests (you may opt out at any time)
  • Send studio newsletters if you have subscribed (opt-out link included in every email)
  • Analyse gameplay to improve game design, performance, and player experience
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • Understand website traffic and improve content
  • Prevent spam, fraud, and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve the site. Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (session management, form security)
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the site (Google Analytics 4)
  • Behaviour analytics: Record anonymised session replays and heatmaps (Microsoft Clarity)
  • Marketing cookies: Track ad campaign performance (Meta Pixel)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling analytics or marketing cookies will not affect core website functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data on our behalf. Each service operates under its own privacy policy:

Service Purpose
Google Analytics 4 Website traffic analysis
Google Tag Manager Tag and tracking script management
Microsoft Clarity Anonymised heatmaps and session recording
Meta Pixel Ad campaign performance tracking
Brevo (Sendinblue) Email marketing and transactional emails
Google Sheets / Google Apps Script Internal lead and beta enquiry management
Unity Technologies (in-app) Game engine, analytics, and crash reporting
Google Play Services (in-app) App distribution, device authentication

5. Data Retention

  • Website form submissions (B2B enquiries): Retained for up to 2 years from submission date, or until you request deletion
  • Beta tester data: Retained for the duration of the beta programme and up to 12 months after the game's public launch
  • Newsletter subscribers: Retained until you unsubscribe
  • Gameplay and analytics data: Retained in aggregated, anonymised form indefinitely; identifiable data deleted within 24 months
  • Crash logs: Retained for 90 days

6. Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@dualcoregames.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and the Reclairos mobile game are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at info@dualcoregames.com and we will delete such data promptly.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include access controls, encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), and limited internal access to personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

DualCore Games is based in India. Your data may be processed and stored by our third-party service providers on servers located outside India, including in the United States and the European Union. By using our website or the Reclairos app, you consent to such transfers. We ensure that our service providers apply appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data protection laws.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website and app may contain links to third-party websites, Discord servers, or app stores. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party services. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or the Reclairos app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
