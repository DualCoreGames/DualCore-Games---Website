Privacy Policy – Reclairos

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Reclairos Terms of Service →About Reclairos →

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DualCore Games ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares information when you play our mobile game Reclairos (the "Game") published on Google Play. It applies to the Game unless a different policy is stated.

By using the Game, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Game.

2. Who we are

Name: DualCore Games

Address: 134/360 Bhari Mandi, Ganesh Ganj, Aminabad, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226018, India

Email: Info@dualcoregames.com

We are the data controller (or "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) for information collected through the Game.

3. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number or contact list.

The Game, and the third-party services built into it, may collect:

Type of dataExamplesWhy
Device and app informationDevice model, operating system version, language, app version, device memoryTo run the Game properly and fix compatibility issues

Your game progress (levels completed, unlocked characters, upgrades, in-game currency, daily rewards, redeemed codes and settings) is saved in a file on your own device. It is not sent to us.

You can buy gems with real money inside the Game. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your card or bank details.

We do not collect precise GPS location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera data.

4. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Save your game progress on your device
  • Deliver in-app purchases (gems)
  • Show advertisements (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads you choose to watch) (for example, to earn energy or gems)
  • Understand how players use the Game so we can improve it
  • Detect crashes, bugs, cheating and fraud
  • Respond to support requests
  • Meet our legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and your choices

Our ad partner (Google AdMob) may use your Advertising ID to show ads and measure their performance. Ads may be personalised based on your activity in our Game and other apps, where the law allows it.

You can limit this at any time:

  • On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and delete or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • You can also contact us at Info@dualcoregames.com with any question about advertising or to ask us to stop using your data.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

7. Children

Our Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Sharing information

We share information only:

  • With the service providers listed in section 5, to run the Game
  • If required by law, court order or a government request
  • To protect our rights, users or the public from fraud or harm
  • With a buyer or successor if our business or the Game is sold or transferred, under this same policy

9. International transfers

We are based in India. Our service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above:

  • Game progress stored on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 14 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the issue is closed.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting laws require.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information
  • Withdraw consent, including for personalised ads
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive your information in a portable format
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights (India)
  • Complain to your data protection authority

These rights come from laws including the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

To use any of these rights, email us at Info@dualcoregames.com with the name of the Game. Because we usually do not know who you are, we may ask for details such as your device model and approximate dates of play to find your data. We will reply within 30 days.

Deleting your data: you can request deletion of your data by emailing Info@dualcoregames.com. Uninstalling the Game removes data stored only on your device.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be at https://dualcoregames.com/privacy-policy/reclairos/, with its effective date at the top. If changes are significant, we will tell you in the Game or on its store listing.
