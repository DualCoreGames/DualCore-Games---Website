Our Concept Art Workflow & Production Pipeline

At DualCore Games, our concept art pipeline serves as the architectural blueprint for the entire visual production. We believe that strong pre-production saves countless hours during the 3D modeling and engine integration phases. Our artists specialize in translating raw gameplay mechanics and narrative themes into concrete visual directions. We begin with extensive reference gathering and mood boards to establish the visual language, followed by rapid thumbnailing to explore silhouettes and composition. Once a direction is approved, we render out high-fidelity orthographics, prop sheets, and material callouts that give our 3D team the exact specifications they need to build engine-ready assets.

Tools & Industry Standard Technologies

Our concept art studio leverages a combination of traditional draftsmanship and modern digital workflows. We primarily utilize Adobe Photoshop and Procreate for 2D illustration and ideation. For complex mechanical designs and architectural layouts, we frequently incorporate 3D blockouts using Blender or Maya before painting over them. This ensures accurate perspective, lighting, and volume right from the concept phase, bridging the gap between 2D illustration and 3D production.

Why Partner With Us?

Unlike traditional illustration studios, our concept artists understand game engines. We design with topology, rigging, and shader limitations in mind. Every character outfit is conceptualized with movement and collision clipping in consideration. Every environment piece is designed for modularity and efficient texture mapping. We don't just create beautiful pictures; we engineer production-ready designs that are built to function seamlessly in real-time engines like Unity and Unreal Engine.
